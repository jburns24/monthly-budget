# Monthly Budget

Shared family spending tracked against per-category limits for each calendar month.

## Language

**Category**:
A user-defined spending bucket within a family.
_Avoid_: Expense category (as a distinct kind of thing — same as Category)

**Monthly Goal**:
The spending limit for one Category in one calendar month for a family. A family may have many Monthly Goals in a month (one per Category); setting or changing one Category’s Monthly Goal does not remove another’s. Removing a Monthly Goal is an explicit action — not implied by leaving a Category blank in a multi-Category edit.
_Avoid_: Budget (when you mean a single Category’s limit)

**Percent of budget used**:
How much of a Category’s Monthly Goal has been spent this month, expressed as a percentage for display when shown at all.
_Avoid_: Percent utilized, utilization
